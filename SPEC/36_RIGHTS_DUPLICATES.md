# 36 — Rights and Duplicate Detection

> **Normative language:** MUST/SHALL = mandatory; SHOULD = recommended unless a documented exception exists; MAY = optional.

## Rights record

Per asset: source, author or provider, acquisition date, licence, commercial permission, modification
permission, attribution requirement and text, restrictions, expiry, evidence reference, content hash, and
for generated assets the `generator_model_id`.

## Status

| Status | Meaning | Autonomous use |
|---|---|---|
| `GREEN` | Commercial use allowed, modification terms known, evidence recorded | Permitted |
| `YELLOW` | Any material term unknown or restricted | Blocked; approval required in MANUAL/ASSISTED |
| `RED` | Prohibited | Blocked in every mode |

`CHECK(status <> 'GREEN' OR (commercial_use = 'ALLOWED' AND modification <> 'UNKNOWN'))`.
GREEN cannot be asserted over an unknown licence — the database refuses it (D-028).

Similarity or inspiration is never treated as permission. An asset that resembles a licensed work is not
thereby licensed, and an asset generated by a model is not thereby free of third-party rights.

## Expiry

A rights record with `expires_at` in the past degrades to `YELLOW`, which invalidates the
`ASSET_GENERATION -> ASSETS_READY` guard for any production still relying on it. Retention holds prevent
collection of assets whose rights evidence is still needed.

## Duplicate detection

| Layer | Method | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Exact | Content hash | Free, catches literal reuse |
| Perceptual | Image and video perceptual hash | Catches re-encodes and crops |
| Transcript | Normalised text similarity | Catches rewritten scripts |
| Semantic | Embedding similarity | Catches restated concepts |
| Structural | Hook and beat-pattern similarity | Catches formula repetition |
| Asset reuse | Cross-production asset hash frequency | Catches over-reliance on one asset |

Thresholds are configurable and versioned; a threshold change is recorded so that a past decision can be
explained under the rules that applied at the time. Exceeding a threshold blocks the autonomous path and
raises a finding rather than silently proceeding.
